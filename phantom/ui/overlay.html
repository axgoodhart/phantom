Recording Options
Choose what to capture, then press SPACE or BEGIN to start recording. While recording, the panel collapses into a small chip — stop anytime with ⌃⌥⌘⎋ (Control‑Option‑Command‑Escape).
⚠ Keystrokes are captured and saved as plain text in the workflow file. Avoid typing passwords or other secrets while recording.
